ℹ️ About NH-44

NH-44 (National Highway 44) is the longest national highway in India, also known as the Golden Quadrilateral of North-South Corridor. It connects the four major metropolitan cities of India and passes through diverse landscapes, cultures, and economies.

Key Facts:

  • Total Length: 4,112 km
  • States Covered: 12 states
  • Major Cities: Srinagar, Delhi, Agra, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Kanyakumari
  • Estd. Driving Time: 59 hours (non-stop)
  • Passes through: Mountains, plains, plateaus, and coastal regions
